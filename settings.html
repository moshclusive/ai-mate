AI Mate Quick Guide

How to Use AI Mate

  • Quick Launch

    Press Alt + A (or Cmd + Shift + A on Mac) anywhere in your browser to instantly open the floating window.

  • Smart Text Selection

    Select any text on a webpage and click the floating icon that appears. Type your instruction or click a saved prompt, and it will be pasted into your selected AI instantly.

  • Send to AI Mate

    Copy an image, take a screenshot, or right-click an image and choose "Send to AI Mate". When the floating window opens, select your prompt. The image and your instruction will be pasted together.

  • Image + Text Combo

    First, copy an image or take a screenshot. Then, select any text on a webpage and click the floating icon. Choose a prompt, and the AI will receive your instruction, the selected text, and the image all at once!

Backup & Restore Prompts

Keep your custom prompt library safe. Export your prompts as a CSV file to back them up, or import a previously saved CSV to restore them on any device.
